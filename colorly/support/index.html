2How do I color?

Pick a color from the palette at the bottom, then swipe your finger across the areas with the matching number. A single tap does not color — just glide over the picture. Pinch to zoom in on small details.

3How do I unlock new sets?

Finish pictures to earn coins and stars, then use the coins to unlock new sets. There are no real-money purchases in the app.

4Where is my progress saved?

Your progress and gallery are stored only on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress, and it does not move between devices.

5Privacy

Colorly works offline, shows no ads and does not collect personal data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
