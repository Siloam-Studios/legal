Privacy Policy

Colorly: Swipe to Color

Effective: November 19, 2026 Last updated: November 19, 2026
We do not collect any personal information. Your coloring stays on your device. 🎨

1Introduction

Welcome to Colorly: Swipe to Color ("the App"), developed and published by Siloam Studios ("we", "us", or "our").

At Siloam Studios, we value your privacy and are committed to providing a safe and enjoyable coloring experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information and protects your privacy.

2Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through the App.

Colorly: Swipe to Color is designed to work without requiring users to provide personal information. The App does not request internet access and does not send any data off your device.

Specifically:

  • We do not require account creation or registration.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • We do not collect location information.
  • We do not collect contacts, photographs, or other personal files.
  • We do not collect payment or financial information.
  • We do not track users' activity across other applications or websites.
  • We do not collect device identifiers or usage analytics.
  • We do not use personal information for advertising or marketing.

You can use the App without providing personal information.

3Game Data and Local Storage

The App stores game-related information, such as coloring progress, completed artwork, and player progress (for example levels and daily pictures), locally on your device.

This information is used solely to provide the App's functionality and maintain your coloring experience.

Locally stored game information is not transmitted to Siloam Studios or stored on our servers.

If you choose to save a finished picture to your phone, the App writes the image file only to the location you select on your device (or to your device's Download folder). This happens only at your request, and the image is not sent to us or to anyone else.

4Device Permissions

The App requests only the permission to use your device's vibration, which provides gentle haptic feedback while coloring. This permission does not give access to any personal information.

5How We Use Information

Since we do not collect personal information through the App, we do not use personal information for advertising, analytics, profiling, or marketing.

Any locally stored game information is used exclusively for the App's functionality.

6Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

The App is distributed through Google Play, which operates independently under Google's own privacy policies and terms. For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit policies.google.com/privacy.

7Data Security

We take user privacy seriously. The App is designed to minimize data processing by not collecting or transmitting personal information.

Game progress is stored locally on the user's device. The security of locally stored information also depends on the device and operating system.

8Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect or store personal information on our servers, we do not maintain a database of users' personal information.

Game data stored by the App can be deleted at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. The App does not use device cloud backup, so uninstalling removes this data. Pictures you chose to save to your device remain there until you delete them.

No user account is required, so there is no account to delete.

9Your Privacy Rights (GDPR)

We respect the privacy rights of users in the European Union and European Economic Area under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Where applicable, individuals have rights relating to their personal data, including the rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, object to processing, and request data portability.

Since the App does not collect personal information, Siloam Studios does not hold personal data associated with your use of the App on which to exercise these rights.

If you contact us by email, we will use the information you provide only to respond to your inquiry and handle any applicable privacy request.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your competent data protection supervisory authority.

10International Data Transfers

The App does not transmit personal information to Siloam Studios or to any third party. Therefore, the App does not initiate international transfers of personal information.

11External Platforms

The App is available through Google Play. Google Play may independently process certain information, such as download information, device details, or transaction information, in accordance with Google's own policies.

Siloam Studios does not control Google's independent data processing activities.

12Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to the App, legal requirements, or our privacy practices. Any changes will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

If we introduce features that collect personal information in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide any disclosures or consent mechanisms required by applicable law before enabling those features.
